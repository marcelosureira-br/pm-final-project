# Persona & Future-State Journey, Module 2

- **Scenario:** StreamLine Spotlight (B2C)

## Persona
- **Role:** A high-frequency, long-tenured subscriber who opens the app by default, not by decision.
- **Goal:** Be watching something worthwhile within minutes — without the search becoming the evening.
- **Friction:** Scrolls 15,000 titles for 20 minutes, closes the app, and reaches for a DVD instead.

## Current workaround (the status quo to beat)
- **External tools:** she ends up going back to a DVD after twenty minutes of scrolling.
- **The process:** Step 1 — Exhaust the in-app path. She opens the app, scrolls for roughly twenty minutes, and closes it without watching anything.

Step 2 — Abandon the platform entirely. She does not switch to a competitor's app or open YouTube (the pattern Raj describes in UXR-08). She goes back to a DVD

Step 3 — Watch something already vetted. A DVD in her possession is, by definition, something she already selected once and presumably liked enough to own. The "workaround" is not really solving tonight's discovery problem — it's opting out of discovery and re-consuming a known quantity.
- **Core frustration:** The moment is when she closes the app without a rejection ever occurring
- **The evidence:** UXR-01 · Priya, 34, heavy viewer (14 yrs)"I open the app, scroll for like twenty minutes, and close it without watching anything. There's 15,000 titles but nothing I actually want. I ended up going back to a DVD."

## Future-state journey map
1. Signal
USER ACTION
Names tonight's mood instead of scrolling the open catalog.
INTERNAL STATE
Expects to be asked a real question, not handed a wall of tiles.
PAIN POINT ADDRESSED
Descriptive search returning nothing usable (BUG-1080).
2. The Editor's Pick
USER ACTION
Reads a short shelf of titles, each with a one-line editorial rationale.
INTERNAL STATE
Feels chosen for by a person, not sorted by a genre engine.
PAIN POINT ADDRESSED
Low-diversity, franchise-repeat recommendations (BUG-1091).
3. The Terminal Yes
USER ACTION
Picks a title from the shortlist — the interface confirms the choice is settled.
INTERNAL STATE
Relief. The evening has a clear starting point instead of an endless maybe.
PAIN POINT ADDRESSED
A scroll with no terminal state — a session that never resolves (BUG-1104, BUG-1121).
4. The Kept Evening
USER ACTION
Watches, finishes satisfied, and opens the app again by habit next time.
INTERNAL STATE
Trust renewed. The subscription is proven worth keeping, again, quietly.
PAIN POINT ADDRESSED
Silent churn risk — loyalty that never shows up in any metric until she leaves.

## Competitive advantages
01
Minutes, not an evening
A curated shortlist replaces a 20-minute scroll plus the walk to the shelf — the whole decision collapses into under a minute.

02
Scale stays usable
She keeps all 15,000 titles as real options, instead of retreating to the few dozen discs she already owns and has already seen.

03
Loyalty becomes visible
Reaching for a DVD leaves no trace in any system. A completed, curated session does — turning silent churn risk into a signal the business can act on.
